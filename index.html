01 About

Five years of production backend work, measured in milliseconds and batch windows.

Five years of Java/Spring and Go microservices for banking and telecom across regional ID, SG, MY, CHN and HKMO markets. Currently at OCBC Indonesia with a dual mandate — core backend engineer on the HKMO team and agentic AI engineer on the Innovation team; previously CIMB Niaga on the CCPL Octo Smart programme and XL Axiata on E-Payment.

Day to day that means backend delivery for the HKMO market, agentic AI development and Model Context Protocol (MCP) integrations, reusable agent skills, a reusable SOA migration workflow, API design with technical architects, rapid prototyping, and cross-border Scrum across South East Asia and China.

The change I am proudest of: a request path from 20 seconds down to ~1.5 seconds by collapsing nested loops into a single pass, which removed the occasional timeouts under concurrent calls — and a 1M-record batch job from ~6 hours down to ~20 minutes. Before that, a B.Eng in Engineering Physics at UGM, where my thesis was emotion identification from thermal-camera CNNs.
